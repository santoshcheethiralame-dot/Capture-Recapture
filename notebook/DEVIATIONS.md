# Deviations from pre-registration

Append-only. Every departure from a frozen pre-registration goes here, the
day it happens, whether it seems small or not. The paper's appendix
reproduces this table. The format follows the standardised deviation table
recommended by Willroth and Atherton (2024).

Deviation types: added analysis, dropped analysis, changed parameter,
changed data or testbed, changed stopping rule, bug fix, exclusion.

| ID | Date | Exp | Pre-registered section | Planned | Done | Type | Reason | Decided before or after seeing confirmatory outcomes? | Expected effect on inference | Results now labelled |
